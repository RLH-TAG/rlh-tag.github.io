## Find a number in Accurx

Royal London Hospital phone numbers, extensions and bleeps are in the Accurx app directory.

<p><a href="https://web.accurx.com/clinician-conversation" class="appbtn" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the Accurx app</a></p>

On an iPhone with Accurx installed, this opens the app at your inbox. From there, open the Directory.

1. Open the Accurx app.
2. Open the **Directory**.
3. If another hospital is showing, select its name, then search for and select **Royal London Hospital**.
4. Search for the contact by name.
